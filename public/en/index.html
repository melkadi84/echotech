Questions

Do you work with clients outside Egypt?

Yes. Websites, apps and business systems are delivered remotely anywhere in the world. Network and CCTV work happens on site, so tell us where you are and we will confirm how we can help.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. After a short discovery conversation we send a written quote with what is included, the timeline and the price.

How long does a project take?

A small website can take a few weeks. Network and CCTV installs depend on the size of the site. You get a timeline in the quote.

Do you support what you build after delivery?

Yes. We offer support and maintenance after handover, and we agree the terms in the quote.

Which languages do you work in?

Arabic and English, for both the work and the interfaces we build.

Will I own my website and accounts?

Domains, hosting and cloud accounts are set up in your name, and we hand over documentation with the project.
