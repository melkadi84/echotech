Data analysis and dashboards

We collect the data you already have, clean it and turn it into dashboards and analysis that answer the questions you ask every week.

Request a quote for this serviceAll services

What's included

Data gathered in one place

Spreadsheets, your systems and exports combined into one clean source, with duplicates and gaps handled.

Dashboards that stay current

A live view of the numbers you track, such as sales, stock, bookings or tickets, that updates without manual work.

Analysis for a real question

Which products earn the most, when you are busiest, where customers drop off. You get findings you can act on, explained plainly.

Reports on a schedule

A weekly or monthly summary delivered by email, in Arabic or English, so you do not have to ask for it.

Access that fits your team

Each person sees the views they need, and sensitive figures stay restricted.

What we need from you to start

  • The questions you want the numbers to answer
  • Where your data lives today (files, systems, exports)
  • Who will read the dashboards and how often
